# Evidence preservation

Preserve evidence close to its original form when source volatility, reacquisition cost, exact wording/schema/measurements, or reinterpretation value justifies it. Follow the repository's archive convention; otherwise use `docs/_research/evidence/<slug>/`. Link the archive from the synthesis, outside the default reading path.

Subject to license, security, and privacy, prefer original exports, measurements, responses, or snapshots over rewritten copies. When full preservation is unsafe or not permitted, retain a stable locator, version and access date, minimal necessary excerpts, and a hash or archive identifier when useful. Keep private transcripts and sensitive data out of public git; record their access class and storage location or preserve redacted/synthetic evidence instead.

Record preserved paths and integrity identifiers in the synthesis's source inventory. Preserve earlier evidence unchanged; add new evidence with its observation date. A source-less copy or a rewrite that could be mistaken for the original is not evidence.
